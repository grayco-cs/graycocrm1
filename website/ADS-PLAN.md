# Google Ads + Local Services Ads plan

For Malcolm and the GrayCo team. Every ad group sends people to the page written for it, so the ad's promise and
the page's headline match. Ad text below is already inside Google's limits (headlines ≤ 30 characters,
descriptions ≤ 90). Keep every claim true. Water emergencies ARE answered 24/7 (owner, 10/27) — use it in the water ads; no prices.

## Before the first dollar

1. **Tracking.** Google Ads → Goals → Conversions → New → Website:
   - "Estimate form sent" (Submit lead form) and "Phone tap" (Phone call lead, clicks on a number on the website).
   - Copy the tag ID (`AW-…`) and the two conversion labels into the website editor: gear → Footer, Google &
     social links. (Or into the CRM: Marketing → 🌐 Website, pixels & guides. The editor wins if both are set.)
   - The website then reports both conversions on every page — the main site and the landing pages.
2. **Auto-tagging on**, and on every campaign this final URL suffix (so each CRM lead shows the campaign):
   `utm_source=google&utm_medium=cpc&utm_campaign={campaignid}`
3. **Connect Google Ads to the CRM:** Marketing → 📈 Ads hub → Connect Google Ads. Spend then lands in the CRM and
   the agency portal by itself, next to the leads and jobs it produced.
4. **CallRail (when ads start):** `docs/CALLRAIL.md`. Put the swap script URL in the website editor.
5. **Location:** People *in* the target area (not "interested in"). Start with Shelby County + DeSoto County.

## Local Services Ads (do this first for water, fire and restoration)

Pay per lead, "Google Guaranteed" badge, shows above search ads. Categories: General Contractor, Water Damage,
Restoration (whichever Google offers in Memphis). Needs: TN license #85034, insurance certificate, owner and
field background checks, and Google reviews. Log the weekly spend in the agency portal → Spend as
"Local Services Ads" — it gets its own line in the monthly report. Dispute leads that are not real jobs.

## Every week

- Search terms report: add negatives, add good terms as keywords.
- Agency portal → Results: cost per lead **and cost per job** by campaign. Google's "conversions" count forms
  and calls; the CRM counts real jobs and revenue. Move budget to the campaigns that win jobs.
- Turn off what costs more per job than the job is worth. Changes to budgets from the CRM go through Approvals.
