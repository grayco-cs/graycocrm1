GrayCo Sub Portal and Bid Site — Privacy Notice

Last updated October 26, 2026

This notice explains what information GrayCo Construction Services, LLC ("GrayCo", 4007 Broadway Rd, Bartlett, TN 38135) collects through the GrayCo bid site, Sub Portal and GrayCo Portal for subcontractors and suppliers, and how we use and protect it. It supplements our general Privacy Policy (privacy.html).

Information we collect

  • Account and company information: company name, owner or contact names, phone, e-mail, address, trades, years in business, contractor license numbers, references.
  • Tax and payment information: your W-9 (which may include an EIN or, for sole proprietors, a Social Security number), payment method and bank details you choose to give us, invoices and payment history.
  • Insurance and compliance documents: certificates of insurance, workers' compensation information, signed agreements and lien waivers.
  • Bids and project information: bids, scopes, prices, questions, schedule and availability, job photos and files you upload.
  • Technical information: device and browser type, IP address, dates and times of sign-in and signing, and basic usage records, for security and to prove what was signed.
  • Messages: messages and replies, including text messages you send or receive.

How we use it

  • To evaluate and award bids, set up and manage your subcontract, schedule work, and pay you.
  • To meet legal and tax duties (for example Form 1099 reporting), insurance and license checks, and lien and payment-bond requirements.
  • To keep the Sub Portal secure, prevent fraud, and keep records of signatures and consent.
  • To communicate with you about jobs, schedules, safety and payments. Promotional texts only if you separately agree.

Who sees it

Your information is available to GrayCo staff who need it. We share it only as needed with: the owner or general contractor on a project (for example your company name, insurance and lien waivers); our service providers that host the system, send e-mail and text messages, handle accounting and payments, and store files, under their own security terms; our insurers, lawyers, accountants and lenders; and government agencies when the law requires. We do not sell your personal information.

Sensitive information

We ask for your tax ID only on your W-9 or payment forms. Do not e-mail a full tax ID or bank account number; upload it in the Sub Portal or give it to the office. Access codes and customer contact details shown to you in the Sub Portal are for the job you are scheduled on only.

How long we keep it

We keep subcontract, payment, tax and insurance records for as long as needed for the work, warranty and lien periods and the legal and tax periods that apply (generally at least seven years), then delete or anonymize them.

Security

We use access controls, encrypted connections and approval steps to protect your information. No system is perfectly secure. Tell us right away if you think your account was misused.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete information we hold about you (we may need to keep some for legal or tax reasons), stop text messages by replying STOP, and ask us not to use your photos publicly. Call (901) 585-0944 or e-mail office@grayco-cs.com. If the law of your state gives you additional rights, we will honor them.

Children

The Sub Portal is for businesses and is not directed to children.

Changes and contact
