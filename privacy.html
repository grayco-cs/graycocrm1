Privacy Policy

Last updated October 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how GrayCo Construction Services ("GrayCo", "we", "us") collects and uses information from customers, subcontractors and visitors, including through our text messaging (SMS) program.

Information we collect

  • Contact details you give us: name, mobile and other phone numbers, e-mail, and property or job address.
  • Job information: estimates, contracts, schedules, photos, invoices and payments related to your project.
  • Messages you send us by text, e-mail or phone, and your text-message consent and opt-out choices.
  • Basic technical information when you use our website or customer portal (such as browser type and pages visited).

How we use it

  • To provide and manage your construction, repair or restoration work: scheduling, updates, estimates, invoices and support.
  • To send text messages you agreed to receive: job notifications, appointment and crew schedule confirmations, and, if you opted in, occasional service offers.
  • To answer your questions and keep records required for our business, insurance claims and legal obligations.

Text messaging (SMS)

If you opt in, GrayCo Construction Services will text you at the number you provided. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out at any time and HELP for help. Consent is not a condition of purchase.

We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. Mobile numbers and SMS consent collected for our text program are never shared with or sold to third parties or affiliates for their marketing. We share information only with service providers that help us run our business (for example, our text-message carrier and software providers) and only as needed to provide our services, or when required by law.

Security and retention

We use reasonable safeguards to protect your information and keep it only as long as needed for the purposes above and our legal and record-keeping obligations.

Your choices

You can opt out of texts at any time by replying STOP, and you can ask us to update or delete your contact information by contacting us below.
