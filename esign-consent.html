Consent to Do Business Electronically

Last updated October 26, 2026

You will see this before you sign anything electronically in the GrayCo portals. Paper copies are always free: call (901) 585-0944.

What this is

GrayCo Construction Services, LLC ("GrayCo") would like to use electronic records and electronic signatures with you for your project. Federal law (the E-SIGN Act, 15 U.S.C. § 7001 et seq.) and the Uniform Electronic Transactions Acts of Tennessee, Mississippi and Arkansas let us do that only if you agree first. Please read this before you sign anything in the portal.

What you are agreeing to

You agree that GrayCo may give you, and you may sign and send back, these things electronically: proposals and estimates, contracts, change orders, work authorizations and directions to pay, lien waivers and notices, invoices and receipts, warranty certificates, completion and walkthrough papers, selections, and notices about your project. Your typed name (and any signature you draw) on a document in the portal is your electronic signature and has the same legal effect as a handwritten signature. GrayCo keeps a record of the date, time, device address (IP address) and what you signed.

This consent applies to this project and to later projects and papers with GrayCo, until you withdraw it.

Your right to a paper copy

You have the right to receive any document on paper, free of charge. To ask for a paper copy, call (901) 585-0944 or e-mail office@grayco-cs.com and tell us which document you want and where to send it. You can also print or save any document you sign in the portal. Where the law requires a notice to be given to you in paper or in a particular form (for example the Notice of Cancellation, which GrayCo gives you in duplicate), GrayCo will give it to you in that form.

Your right to say no, and to withdraw your consent

You do not have to agree to do business electronically. If you do not agree, GrayCo will give you paper documents and sign them with you in person or by mail, and your price and your rights are the same. If you agree now, you may withdraw your consent at any time by calling (901) 585-0944 or e-mailing office@grayco-cs.com (or by writing to GrayCo, 4007 Broadway Rd, Bartlett, TN 38135). Withdrawing consent does not affect any document you already signed electronically. It may delay your project because paper documents will have to be mailed or signed in person. There is no fee for withdrawing consent.

What you need

  • A computer, tablet or phone with an internet connection.
  • A current web browser (the latest two versions of Chrome, Safari, Edge or Firefox) with JavaScript and cookies turned on.
  • A program that can open PDF files (such as the free Adobe Acrobat Reader, or the PDF viewer built into your phone or browser).
  • An e-mail address and/or a mobile phone number that you can use to receive your personal portal link and your signed copies.
  • A way to print or save PDF copies if you want them.

If our requirements change in a way that could keep you from opening your documents, we will tell you before the change and you may withdraw your consent without charge.

Keeping your information current

Tell GrayCo right away if your e-mail address, mobile number or mailing address changes so we can keep sending your documents to you.

Your copies

After you sign, a signed copy is stored in your portal and e-mailed to you. Keep a copy for your records.
