## 1. Lag and idle churn with real data (priority 1)

**What was wrong.** Once Estimates had been opened, every other tab felt slow, and stayed slow until a reload.
- The Estimate Builder kept its hidden **Rate Book** (658 rows, about 11,000 page elements) and **Price Log** (950 items,
  about 14,000 elements) drawn inside the page, even when you weren't on those builder tabs or in the builder at all.
- The page went from about 3,000 elements to about 31,000.
- Dozens of small background checks walk the whole page every 1–2 seconds, so each one had 10× more to walk.
- The main thread was busy about 40% of the time while nobody touched anything: 12.0 s of long tasks in 30 s idle.

**Fixed (SWEEP2), measured with a CPU profile on the same data:**

| | before (f72bb51) | after |
|---|---|---|
| page elements after visiting Estimates | 31,789 | 6,779 (6,826 on 48ab390) |
| long tasks, 30 s idle on the Dashboard after Estimates | 12.0 s (91) | 6.0 s (45) on the SWEEP2 build; 8.5 s (69) on 48ab390, which runs more background sync |

- **Rate Book / Price Log:** each list is drawn only while its own builder tab is showing, and emptied when you leave it.
  Opening the tab draws it, so nothing is lost.
- **Accounting register:** each row's job box holds only its own job. The full job list (about 150 jobs) goes in the moment the
  box is pressed, focused or touched. Before, every one of about 90–190 rows carried every job.
- **Draft guardian:** "restore what you were typing" no longer searches the whole page again for every empty field every
  1.5 s. It does nothing when there is no recent draft.
- **E-mail icon scan:** it now walks the page's text only when the page actually changed, instead of every 1.5 s.
- **CompanyCam panel tick:** a full-page text search every 2 s whose answer was never used is gone.
- **Repeated writes:** the "Saved ✓" badge and the Approvals count stop rewriting the same value every tick.

**Tests.** `test_SWEEP2.mjs` scores 10/10 on the SWEEP2 build and on 48ab390, and 5/10 on f72bb51. It checks:
- the builder lists draw only on their own tab;
- the page stays small;
- the register job box fills when pressed, and reassigning a job still works;
- there are no page errors.
