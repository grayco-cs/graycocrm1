Common questions

What if we find something behind the walls?
Rot, mold or structural issues are communicated immediately with photos and options. Extra work is done only with a signed Change Order.
How do payments work?
Progress draws tied to milestones defined in your contract. We invoice you at each milestone; final payment follows the final walkthrough.
Who handles permits?
GrayCo handles permit applications unless otherwise noted in your contract.
What is the Checks & Balances sheet?
A one-page record we leave on site. Each stage — pre-start, daily, mid-job, punch list and final walkthrough — is dated and initialed by your PM and reviewed by our office.
Can I see progress when I’m not home?
Yes — daily progress photos are available on CompanyCam.
Written warranty

Workmanship warranty: 1 year from completion, plus manufacturer warranties on materials. Your warranty certificate is issued at closeout. Roofs: 5 years. To file a claim: (901) 585-0944 or office@grayco-cs.com — we respond within 24 hours.
