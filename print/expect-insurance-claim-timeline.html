Typical durations

  • Water: emergency mitigation 1–3 days; structural drying 3–5 days (monitored daily until dry standards are met); rebuild per approved scope.
  • Fire: board-up Day 1; mitigation weeks 1–2; engineer review if needed weeks 2–3; then rebuild.
  • Supplements for hidden damage can extend the timeline.

Honest about your deductible

We never ask you to inflate or skip your deductible — and we never offer to “cover” it. Your out-of-pocket cost is limited to your deductible unless you approve extra work in writing.

Paperwork you’ll see

  • Work Authorization (3-day right to cancel)
  • Our estimate & the insurer’s scope
  • Supplements, with photos
  • Change Orders for out-of-scope work
  • Certificate of Completion & Satisfaction
  • Warranty certificate & lien waiver

Your insurance company decides what your policy covers. GrayCo documents the damage, communicates with your adjuster and keeps you informed — you will receive copies of what we submit.
