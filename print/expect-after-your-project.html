Caring for your project

General tips — always follow the manufacturer care guides we leave with you.

  • Roofs: keep gutters and valleys clear; check from the ground after major storms — please don’t walk on the roof.
  • Tile & showers: use non-abrasive cleaners; keep caulk joints in good shape and ventilate after showers.
  • Paint & drywall: let new paint cure before scrubbing; keep leftover paint for touch-ups.
  • Floors: use felt pads under furniture; clean spills promptly.
  • After water damage: tell us right away about any new staining, odor or soft spots.
  • Commercial: report tenant modifications that affect our work before they happen.
