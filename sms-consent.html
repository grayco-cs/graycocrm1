Text Message Consent (SMS Opt-In)

Last updated October 26, 2026

Customers and subcontractors opt in to GrayCo Construction Services text messages in writing on our signed contract or job intake form (paper or e-signed). The form has a mobile number field and two separate checkboxes, both unchecked by default. Job texts and promotional texts are separate choices; checking one does not check the other, and neither is required to do business with us.

People who text our number first are opted in to job texts only (never promotional texts), and our first reply says: "GrayCo Construction Services: you're subscribed to job texts. Msg freq varies. Msg&data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help, STOP to opt out."
