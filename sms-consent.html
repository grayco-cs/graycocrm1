Text Message Consent (SMS Opt-In)

Last updated October 26, 2026

Customers and subcontractors opt in to GrayCo Construction Services text messages on our signed contract or job intake form (paper or e-signed). The form includes a mobile number field and this separate checkbox, which is unchecked by default:

Mobile number for texts: (___) ___-____

☐ I agree to receive text messages from GrayCo Construction Services about my job (scheduling, updates, estimates, invoices) and occasional service offers. Message frequency varies. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out, HELP for help. Consent is not a condition of purchase.

People who text our number first are also opted in, and our first reply says: "GrayCo Construction Services: you're subscribed to job texts. Msg freq varies. Msg&data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help, STOP to opt out."
